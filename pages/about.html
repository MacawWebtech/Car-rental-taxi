Our story

Kaarvan began with a late-night airport pickup that never arrived. Two engineers, stuck at MAA at 3 a.m., decided there had to be a better way to book a car.

Nine years later we run a fleet of 2,800 vehicles and 3,400 driver-partners across eight cities — but the rule from that first night hasn't changed: if we promise a time, we keep it.

Nine years later, we operate a fleet of 2,800 vehicles and work with 3,400 driver-partners across eight cities. From early-morning airport transfers to long-distance road trips, business travel and everyday rides, we’ve built Kaarvan around the moments when getting there matters most.

Kaarvan began with a late-night airport pickup that never arrived. Two engineers, stuck at MAA at 3 a.m., decided there had to be a better way to book a car — one where reliability wasn’t something you had to hope for.

What started as a simple idea grew into a mobility service built around one promise: when you book with us, your journey should feel effortless from the moment you confirm it.

We believe great mobility is about more than cars. It’s about clean vehicles, professional drivers, transparent pricing, easy bookings and support when you need it. Every journey is an opportunity to make travel simpler, smoother and more dependable.

Why we started

India's mobility market was fast but inconsistent. We wanted the reliability of a chauffeur service at the convenience of an app — and pricing you could explain to your finance team.
